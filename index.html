What this is — and what it is not yet

LFP Atlas v0.1 is a seeded edition. It restructures a compiled research workbook (19 July 2026) into the same claim architecture as its sibling NMC Atlas: one canonical JSON file, every figure a discrete claim with its own source, basis, scope and chemistry tag, and a build that fails if any displayed figure lacks a citation. What it does not yet have is NMC Atlas's evidence depth: claims currently inherit row-level seed sources, most lack vintages, and no source is snapshot-archived. Every one of those weaknesses is listed in the gaps register below — shipped openly, not smoothed over. The v0.2 work programme is figure-level re-sourcing to the NMC standard.

What the labels mean

kind — capacity (nameplate), shipments, output, cumulative output, a rate in native units, a market share, or a qualitative claim. Only capacity can ever count toward a total; shipments and output are market context.
basis — built, reported (stated as current, vintage unclear), construction, announced, planned, target, historic or cancelled. Targets count toward nothing: expansion goals often subsume current figures, and adding them would double-count.
chem — LFP, LMFP, L(M)FP-unsplit (the document doesn't split them), cathode-unsplit (broader cathode figure), FePO₄ (precursor — never in CAM totals), or not-LFP.
scope — site, company or group; site-scope pipeline claims add up, company-scope claims take the maximum.
bundle — the stated figure includes non-CAM tonnage (e.g. "LFP + iron-phosphate"); rides in the upper bound only.

Seed provenance

Sixty-five producer rows (including precursor-only, exited, and cell-maker context rows — greyed, counting nothing), 28 synthesis methods in five families, 18 patent & licensing events and 10 backbone references, from LFP_LMFP_Synthesis_Methods_and_Producers.xlsx (compiled 19 July 2026). The workbook's own evidence policy: no sources, companies, patents or numbers were fabricated; each row carries a confidence rating and a source. Geography is honest about its own precision: where the seed states only a province or country, the dot says so.
