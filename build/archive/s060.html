Company Profile

Chongqing Terui Battery Materials Co., Ltd.

 

With the vigorous development of the new energy industry, Chongqing Terui Battery Materials Co., Ltd. came into being to contribute to the construction of a green, civilized, and advanced modern city. Since its establishment in 2007, Chongqing Terui has always focused on the research and development, production and sales of environmentally friendly new energy lithium battery cathode materials, and has won recognition in the industry for its high-quality products.
Thanks to the country’s support for the new energy industry, Chongqing Terui, relying on Shenzhen Nanfang Tongzheng Investment Co., Ltd., is continuously strengthening its own strength. In 2016, Chongqing Terui New Energy Materials Co., Ltd. was established. , Chongqing Tianhai Battery Material Co., Ltd.
In 2017, the first phase of the "100,000 tons of lithium ion battery cathode material project" with a total investment of more than 3 billion yuan was officially put into operation, with a total production capacity of more than 20,000 tons per year. The products include lithium iron phosphate battery materials XC116, XC333 and XC555 , Lithium nickel cobalt manganese oxide (ternary material) TR202 and TR610, TR100, TR200, TR300 and many other categories. In November of the same year, the second phase of the iron phosphate project officially started construction. After the completion of the project, there will be 48 lithium iron phosphate production lines with an annual production capacity of 60,000 tons. In addition, the planning of the third phase of the project has been put on the agenda.
Carefully crafted, we are down to earth; with the world in mind, we have lofty ambitions. Facing the future, we have only one goal: to take social responsibility as our own responsibility, continuously improve our own strength, and build an outstanding brand in China's lithium battery industry! We are convinced that Terry, as it matures day by day, will move forward with a more steady pace, with a sense of urgency and a sense of mission that don't wait for me. Adhering to the concept of integrity and openness, our company is willing to work hand in hand with all sectors of the society to create a more brilliant tomorrow!
