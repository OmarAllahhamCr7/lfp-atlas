Lithium Iron Phosphate

Products Introduction

Phosphoric acid products offer stable, low-cost raw materials essential for the global push toward new energy and net-zero emissions.

Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP), currently the world’s most widely mass-produced cathode material, is expected to gradually shift toward Lithium Manganese Iron Phosphate (LMFP) in applications requiring higher energy density, such as electric vehicles.

Phosphoric acid-based products are nano-scale composite materials and thus possess a broad range of physical and electrical properties. They can be developed into various forms and specifications tailored to specific end-use requirements, satisfying the needs of both battery manufacturers and end customers.
