VSPC Advanced Cathode Powder Manufacturing Demonstration Project

  • $30m Funded by ARENA
  • $60.01m Total project cost
Project overview
  • Lead Organisation

    VSPC Pty Ltd

    Location

    Queensland

    ARENA Program

    Advancing Renewables Program

  • Start date

    1 February 2025

    End date

    31 March 2029

  • Project Partners
    Lycopodium Ltd

Summary

VSPC will commercialise its advanced cathode powder manufacturing process by constructing a 250 tonne per annum (tpa) demonstration facility.

Need

Currently, cathode active material (CAM) manufacturing for lithium ferro phosphate (LFP) and lithium manganese ferro phosphate (LMFP) batteries is almost exclusively located in China. Chinese manufacturers generally use either solid or solution-phase synthesis methods.

VSPC has developed a proprietary and patented CAM manufacturing process (i.e. the RC Process) which is able to combine the advantages of both the solid-state and solution-phase synthesis methods to produce LFP and LMFP powders. The RC Process has greater flexibility in control of product characteristics and quality, reduced manufacturing costs, and reduced waste in comparison to typical Chinese processes.

Action

VSPC will design, construct and operate a 250 tpa LFP cathode powder Demonstration Plant Australia to produce samples for customer testing and qualification in line with the customer requirements.

During the Project, VSPC will produce pre-qualification samples of LFP CAM for potential customers and investors. The objective of the Project is to secure a offtake agreements to support funding for a future commercial project (c.25,000 tpa) or to license the technology to other CAM, chemical or battery manufacturers.

Outcome

The Project will demonstrate a new process for producing CAM for both LFP and LMFP battery chemistries.
