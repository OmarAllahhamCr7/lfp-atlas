About

Trusted LFP manufacturer under license from LiFePO4+C Licensing AG

LiFeP04 offers the best cell performance and highest safety standards for lithium-ion batteries used in automotive and stationary storage applications.

With more than 10 years of technical know-how in the field of cathode materials, and thanks to its joint-venture with the Chinese group Pulead, Prayon is a trusted partner that offers non-counterfeiting, high-quality and reliable LiFeP04 cathode materials to lithium-ion battery producers across the globe.

Secure your freedom to operate

The manufacturing and the sale of LFP is conducted under a worldwide license from LiFePO4+C Licensing AG.

As a licensee, Prayon gives you the assurance that the use as well the applications of the LFP is fully compliant with the license and avoids any infringement that could have a major business impact, as patent owners are allowed to claim for a seizure in the event of counterfeiting.

Avoid the risks of seizure for counterfeiting or distraint-description

By choosing Prayon as a partner, you can acquire LFP in full confidence and avoid any risk of a “distraint-description” procedure or a seizure, as the owner has the right to prohibit the use of counterfeit materials.
