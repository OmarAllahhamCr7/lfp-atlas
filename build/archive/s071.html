Diversified technologies

Coordinated industrial layout enables comprehensive competitive advantages in the industrial chain

  • series of ternary precursors

    Precursors such as NCM523, NCM622, NCM811, NCA, and NCM90, among which single crystal and high nickel precursors are the key core materials for preparing the cathode of ternary lithium battery

    Performance characteristics：high energy density, high safety and long life
    Application field：transportation power field and energy storage field

  • High voltage cobaltosic oxide

    It is divided into cobalt oxide doped with large particles and small particles, which is the core material for preparing lithium cobaltate batteries

    Performance characteristics：high voltage, high power
    Application field：3C field

  • Binary and multicomponent precursors

    NC and NM series binary products are precursors of new high nickel and cobalt-free battery cathode materials. Multicomponent precursors prepared by doping can significantly improve battery performance

    Performance characteristics：high voltage, high energy density and high safety
    Application field：power transportation, electric tools

  • Iron phosphate

    Core material for preparation of the cathode of lithium iron phosphate batteries

    Performance characteristics：Performance characteristics: high cycle, thermal stability and low cost
    Application field：Application fields: power transport and energy storage

  • LFP

    Thefourthgenerationproductindependentlydeveloped,withhighvoltagedensityreachingtheindustry'sadvancedlevel.

    Performance characteristics：effectively reduce costs and high voltage density
    Application field：EV, energy storage, etc.

  • Sodium-ion battery precursors

    Layered oxide,polyanion and other precursors

    Performance characteristics：Performance characteristics: long cycle, high safety and high rate
    Application field：Application fields: power-driven transport and energy storage
